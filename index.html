About

I'm a third-year Applied Mathematics student at Universitas Pelita Harapan, focusing on financial engineering with a foundation in financial mathematics, stochastic processes, statistical modeling, and data analysis.

My academic interests sit at the intersection of mathematical theory and real-world financial application — from regression analysis and risk theory to investment analysis and data-driven decision making. I'm drawn to problems where quantitative reasoning creates clearer insight.

Outside of coursework, I tutor mathematics privately, co-coordinate my university's mathematics student association, and organize events around stock trading and investment education. I have co-authored academic work published in IEEE and EKOMA, developed a Satria Data 2025 Statistics Essay Competition paper applying XGBoost and SHAP to social welfare allocation, and completed an LSTM-based portfolio optimization study across LQ45, DJIA, SSE50, and a Global portfolio.
