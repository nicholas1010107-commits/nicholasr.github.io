Sky Galley 3D

You're the cabin crew, walking the aisle of a real plane.

  • Cook in the galley at the back of the plane and load dishes onto your cart.
  • Walk forward down the aisle. Passengers sit on both sides; their order floats above their head.
  • Get close and tap a passenger to serve them.
  • Food keeps cooking (and burning!) while you're away. Angry passengers make their row angrier, and walk-outs cost ❤️.
